## Journal correction

Journal cards and draft article leads now use original code-native SVG concept illustrations: specialist (consultation record), HydraFacial (generic handpiece/hydration), acne (texture/tone study), laser (handpiece/follicle study), PRP (tube/hair study). These are labelled conceptual editorial illustrations, not anatomical instructions or actual procedures/results. Their source files are in public/illustrations and statically imported in lib/media.ts for fingerprinted caching. No third-party photographs are used for the five article topic visuals.

## Owner-supplied clinic photography

The homepage features the user-supplied retouched reception. The gallery includes the reception, seating area and two treatment-room views. The seating and treatment-room edits were made with built-in ImageGen using the owner photos as references. The edits tidy incidental clutter and balance lighting in warm ivory, taupe, muted mauve and blue while retaining the room layout and equipment. The brochure informed the text-only practitioner profiles. The photo showing a security monitor was left out of the public gallery.
